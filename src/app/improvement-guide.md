Alright Tina, I've gone through moneycho.com. It's clean and credible, but it's leaving a lot on the table for conversions and stickiness. Here's the breakdown:

---

**🎨 Eye-Catching (Design & First Impression)**

- Your hero headline "Smart Money Starts with Better Math" is clever but vague. A visitor in 3 seconds doesn't know _what_ they'll get. Try something benefit-first: "Calculate Exactly How to Save, Invest & Pay Off Debt, Free."
- No visual above the fold. Add one hero illustration or a mini interactive calculator preview right there. People scan visually before they read.
- The stat badges (6+ calculators, 100% free, etc.) are good trust signals but they blend in. Give them a slight background card or subtle animation on scroll.
- Your topic grid and calculator list look identical in hierarchy. Differentiate them more, maybe make calculators larger cards with a preview screenshot.

---

**🔍 SEO**

- Title tag is way too long and keyword-stuffed ("Free Financial Calculators, Budget Tools & Money Guides | MoneyCho"). Trim to ~55 chars: "Free Financial Calculators & Money Guides | MoneyCho"
- You need dedicated landing pages per calculator with unique H1s targeting long-tail queries like "compound interest calculator monthly contributions" or "50/30/20 budget calculator." These are high-intent, low-competition keywords.
- Your guides section is gold for SEO but I don't see internal linking between guides and calculators. Every guide should link to 1-2 relevant calculators and vice versa.
- Add FAQ schema markup at the bottom of each guide/calculator page. Google loves surfacing those in SERPs for financial queries.
- Missing: a blog/insights index page with proper category URLs (/guides/budgeting/, /guides/investing/) for topical authority.

---

**📩 Lead Generation**

- You have **zero capture mechanism** right now. "No sign-up" is your value prop, which is great for trust, but you still need an opt-in. Options:
  - "Email me my results" after someone uses a calculator (natural, high-intent moment)
  - A free PDF ("Your Personal Finance Starter Kit") gated behind email
  - A "Get monthly rate updates" newsletter signup in the footer or after the insights section
- Add a sticky bottom bar or exit-intent popup with a single compelling offer. Keep it low-friction: just email, no name.
- The CTA buttons ("Try Our Calculators" / "Read Free Guides") go to content but never capture. Add a secondary CTA that captures.

---

**🔁 User Retention**

- No account, no saved results, no reason to come back. Let users bookmark/save their calculations (even just via localStorage with a "share this result" link).
- Add a "What's new" or changelog section so returning users see fresh content.
- The "Updated Monthly" badge is a promise. Make it visible with actual dates on the homepage ("Last updated: June 24, 2026").
- Consider a free email course: "5 Days to a Smarter Budget" that drips content and drives them back to calculators each day.
- Add related calculators/guides at the bottom of every tool page ("You used the budget calculator, now try the savings goal calculator").

---

**Priority order if you want max impact fast:** Lead capture on calculator results → SEO landing pages per tool → hero redesign with visual → email drip for retention.
